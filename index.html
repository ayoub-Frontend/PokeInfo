Our Gallery

1. Prologue: The Journey Before The Journey
1380

The Journey Before The Journey

Ash went on a Pokemon adventure in the forest when he was just a little boy. He got lost and took shelter under a tree along with some Pokemon, inspiring his dream to become a Pokemon trainer.

The Beginning

Ash was just 10 when he started his incredible Pokemon journey! He overslept, missed his first choice of Pokemon, and ended up with Pikachu, who didn’t like him at first and shocked him.

3. Gold & Silver
1385

Johto League

Ash collected five Gym Badges by battling Gym Leaders like Falkner and Bugsy. He even caught a shiny Noctowl and fought with his old friend Misty. Sadly, he also had to say goodbye to his Charizard, leaving it at the Charicific Valley to train.

4. Ruby and Sapphire

I. Hoenn

The next leg of Ash’s journey took him to the Hoenn region, where he met May, and Max and Brock also returned. May wanted to be a Pokemon coordinator, so Ash helped her as much as possible while exploring the region.

5. Diamond and Pearl
1895

I. Sinnoh

Ash went to Sinnoh, leaving his new captures at Professor Oak’s lab except for Aipom, who snuck along. He reunited with Brock and got a new travel buddy, Dawn, who wanted to be a Pokemon coordinator like her mom.

6. Black and White

I. Unova

Ash continued his journey through Unova; he caught some cool new Pokemon like Oshawott, Tepig, and Snivy and won a bunch of badges, including the trio badge, basic badge, and insect badge. He made it to the Unova Pokemon League and placed in the top eight.
